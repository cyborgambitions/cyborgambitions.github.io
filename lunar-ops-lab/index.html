01 · The stack

Eight sentences. Then sit down.

  1. Earth is a delay. The Moon is a job site.
  2. A rocket is infrastructure. A stock price is a vote on who owns the bottleneck.
  3. Cadence without contracts is a story.
  4. Contracts without cadence is a spreadsheet.
  5. A customer is someone with money allocated — NASA, DoD, a constellation, hyperscale. A hypothetical customer is a mood.
  6. If you cannot name the next funded event in quarters, you do not have a thesis. You have a poster.
  7. You get $2,500 of paper. Same size as a family experiment. Not a hedge fund costume.
  8. You do not “invest.” You sit a Monday lock. Educational only. Not advice.

Name does not enter unless the gate ≥ 3.

Green = 1 · Yellow = 0.5 · Red = 0. Four names max. Most books should fail. That is the lesson.

04 · Monday lock script

Teacher reads this like a flight director.

  1. 0:00 Pens down. Books are open. No phones as oracles.
  2. 0:01 “You have twelve minutes. Gate every name. If it is under 3, it does not enter. Cash is allowed.”
  3. 0:08 “Write the thesis in one sentence. If you need a paragraph, you do not have a thesis.”
  4. 0:11 “Add. The line must be 2,500. If it is not, you are not locked.”
  5. 0:12 “Lock. Fold the card. No edits until Friday. That is the whole religion.”

Optional 10 min: AETHER card — deconstruct “we’re going back to the Moon.” Fundamentals, hidden assumptions, rebuild. No speeches.

07 · Teacher key

Answers are process. Not tickers.

  • You are not grading whether they picked RKLB. You are grading whether the gate was honest.
  • A book of four greens that cannot name a customer is a fail.
  • A book of cash + one name with a real contract is a pass.
  • If everyone “wins,” the gate is too soft. Tighten Q3 and Q5.
  • Do not let them trade Friday. The lock is the product.
  • If a kid asks “is this advice?” you say no, then you point at the stamp on every sheet.
